## Content, sales, and marketing

- **Content and SEO:** The Hương Camera series demonstrates [content clusters in EzyArticle](https://ezyplatform.com/blog/internal-link-va-cum-noi-dung-tren-ezyarticle?lang=vi). Link a pillar article to supporting articles and link back from them. Link comparisons to product pages when that matches search intent. Address [technical SEO](https://ezyplatform.com/blog/technical-seo-co-ban-cho-website-ezyplatform?lang=vi) first: images, mobile display, the canonical domain, and the sitemap. Do not confuse [Search Console with EzyRating](https://ezyplatform.com/blog/do-hieu-qua-seo-bang-ezyrating-va-search-console?lang=vi): Google clicks, sessions, funnel drop-off, and channel revenue answer different questions. Compare each metric with itself over time, then pick one improvement. The figures in the measurement article are illustrative.
- **Retail:** The [fashion sale landing page](https://ezyplatform.com/blog/landing-page-dot-sale-shop-thoi-trang?lang=vi) shows E-Commerce managing prices and stock while the landing page directs visitors to the sale collection through a single CTA destination. Make sure “from” and “up to” prices do not include out-of-stock items; check mobile behavior, channel-specific UTMs, the funnel to purchase, and what happens after the sale ends. The [fashion lead magnet](https://ezyplatform.com/blog/qua-tang-de-khach-de-lai-thong-tin-shop-thoi-trang?lang=vi) comes first to collect information from people who have not bought. It is not permission to send promotional emails to an entire list.
  - In the demo, the sale page is an EzyArticle `POST` with an `lp-…` slug. A custom theme renders it as a noindex landing page, and two lines of article metadata set the expiration and the expired-sale message. This is not an automatic convention in every theme. Use the same `utm_campaign` to group the sale, and `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_content` to distinguish channels or content variants.

## Industry case studies

- **Consulting and customer care:** [VICCA Law](https://ezyplatform.com/blog/ezyplatform-da-ho-tro-luat-vicca-tu-dong-hoa-mot-so-quy-trinh-nhu-the-nao?lang=vi) describes taking website inquiries, classifying customers in EzyCRM, and sending notifications through EzyOA (Zalo or Telegram). Keep customer data, processing status, and notification channels distinct, and check read permissions, message content, and per-channel configuration.
- **Creative communities:** [Vietales](https://ezyplatform.com/blog/ezyplatform-da-giai-quyet-van-de-tu-dong-hoa-cho-mang-xa-hoi-cua-vietales-nhu-the-nao-?lang=vi) demonstrates classifying work, providing an editor for authors, running contests with criteria and scoring, and separating interface changes from article management. Identify reusable data and content separately from workflows and permissions specific to the community.
- **Logistics:** [Vạn Lý Bảo Tiêu](https://ezyplatform.com/blog/vi-sao-hang-tu-trung-quoc-ve-viet-nam-nhanh-nhu-noi-dia-goc-nhin-tu-he-thong-cua-van-ly-bao-tieu?lang=vi) covers cross-border shipping. Read related articles to distinguish shipment status, accounting, integrations, notifications, and affiliate marketing. The implementation account does not guarantee delivery times or establish a shared core API.
- **Training:** [HTC Academy](https://ezyplatform.com/blog/hanh-trinh-EzyPlatform-trien-khai-thanh-cong-cho-website-cua-htc-academy?lang=vi) and the e-learning articles are starting points for courses, content, enrollment and payments, and student operations. Do not assume every part of the system described in the case study is installed in the stack.
- **Games:** The [EzySmashers series](https://ezyplatform.com/blog/tim-hieu-tro-choi-ezysmashers-phan-10-tao-du-an-tren-ezyplatformcom-va-phat-hanh-mot-phien-ban?lang=vi) covers game structure, asset adjustments, builds, project creation, and releases. Distinguish Unity steps from decisions about Socket integration, runtime operations, and Java plugin releases.

Case studies describe what one project did; guides describe a workflow in a particular configuration. Before reproducing either, check the environment, permissions, module documentation, and current versions instead of assuming that the demo still applies.
